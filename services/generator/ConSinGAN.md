# Objective
Since 2022 an interest for learning generative models from a single image, as oppossed to from a large dataset has been grown. This service has the task of generating images when a __new defect__ has been detected, or simply to augment the training data for YOLO.


Reference [Improved techniques for training simple-image GANS](https://www.tobiashinz.com/2020/03/24/improved-techniques-for-training-single-image-gans.html).
